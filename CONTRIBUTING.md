# Contributing

Claude Walk is a personal tool, shared as is. Contributions are welcome, with honest
expectations:

- **Issues** are the best way to help. Please include your OS, phone model and Android
  version, your Claude Code version (`claude --version`), and what `/health` reports (with
  the token removed).
- **Pull requests** are welcome but may be slow to get a look. For anything larger than a
  fix, open an issue first so we can agree it fits the design in
  [docs/how-it-works.md](docs/how-it-works.md).
- Keep `cd bridge && bun smoke.ts` passing, and follow the rules in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).
  They apply to people as much as to agents.
- Security problems: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the issue tracker.
